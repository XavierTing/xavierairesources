Humanizer

Deletes the AI accent from your writing.

Why

Em-dash chains, “it's not X, it's Y”, bold on everything: readers smell AI prose instantly. Humanizer strips 33 documented patterns of machine writing and can calibrate to your voice from samples of your own work.

When

The last pass before anything goes public: posts, documentation, emails, portfolio copy.

How

  1. Install it as a skill or Claude Code plugin.
  2. Run /humanizer on pasted text or point it at a file.
  3. Feed it a few writing samples once, so rewrites keep your voice.
npx skills add blader/humanizer --global
